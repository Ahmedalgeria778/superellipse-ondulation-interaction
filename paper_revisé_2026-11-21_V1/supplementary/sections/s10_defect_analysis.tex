The variation of the defect between $N=128$ and the finest grid is $\Delta\varepsilon_E \approx 5.0$ percentage points at $Re=100$ (over $N=128$--$768$) and $\approx 5.3$ points at $Re=400$ (over $N=128$--$768$), while the contrast $I_D(2,4)$ varies by only $7.2\times10^{-4}$~pp and $2.4\times10^{-3}$~pp, respectively---i.e., three to four orders of magnitude less. The defect is therefore a common-mode resolution error of the absolute integrals $P^*$ and $D^*$ that cancels in their paired difference. The small residual $\Delta\varepsilon_E$ between $n=2$ and $n=4$ at fixed $N$ (third column) is at most $0.011\%$, which bounds the contamination of the contrast by differential defect effects to well below the quoted span.
